Read before you order stools

One stairwell. At −7.60 m with 200 people, this room has a single protected escape route plus a car lift. Assembly use normally needs two. Get it answered by Junó's fire engineer first — it outranks everything else here.

The bar now has room to work. 2.8 m of clear standing in front of the counter, 16 m², plus a 1.3 m route running east to the dancefloor. No table is within 2.8 m of the bar.

Tables are set at 1.50 m each — 70 cm top with 40 cm either side. That is party density: stools tucked, people perching. It is what makes 30 fit.

Confirm the column before setting out. Position is read off the garage plan at 1:100 and matched to your photos; 30 cm either way changes row C.

The drainage channel runs under row E and the standing zone. Flush cover plate or carpet, or stool feet catch in the grating.
